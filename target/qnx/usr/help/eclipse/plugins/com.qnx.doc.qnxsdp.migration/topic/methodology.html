Planning Your Migration

What you have to do to migrate to the current version of QNX SDP depends on where you're coming from. Items to consider include:

  • migrating the BSP (go to Board Support Packages)
  • new compiler issues (go to Toolchain)
  • discontinued items (go to the QNX Software Development Platform 8.0: Deprecation and Discontinuation Notice), and
  • new security features (go to Security).
